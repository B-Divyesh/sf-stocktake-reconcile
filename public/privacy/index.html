Privacy

Your inventory CSV, physical counts, reasons, notes, adjustment files, and reports remain in this browser or desktop app unless you export a file yourself.

Stocktake Reconcile does not include analytics, advertising, telemetry, a hosted inventory database, or release lookups. The sample demo uses temporary in-memory data and is discarded when you start a real count.

Contact the product repository owner with privacy questions.
