Privacy

Stocktake Reconcile is local-first. Your inventory CSV, physical counts, reasons, notes, adjustment journals, and reports stay on your device unless you choose to export a file.

The app does not include analytics, advertising, telemetry, or a hosted inventory database. The landing page may request GitHub’s public release manifest to show the current installer. If you choose Pro Archive, your license token is stored in this browser or app and is checked with Sociobot’s billing API no more than once a day when connected. No inventory data is sent with that check.

Sociobot/Dodo processes purchases as merchant of record. Their checkout privacy terms apply to purchase information. Contact the operator through the product repository for privacy questions.
